Jump to your other display. Keep typing.

HopFocus moves your pointer and your keyboard focus together. Press one shortcut and you land where you were working on that display, in the app you were using, ready to type.

The problem

With two or more displays, switching means finding the pointer, dragging it across, then clicking into the window before you can type again. Tools that jump the pointer save the drag, but you still have to click first.

How it works

  1. You are typing in an app on your external display. Press ⌃1.
  2. The pointer lands on your built-in display at the exact spot you last used there, with a brief highlight so you see where.
  3. The app you were using on that display is active again. Keep typing; no click needed.
  4. Press ⌃2 and you are back in the app on the external display, just as you left it.

Focus on Switch, without permissions

Bringing the right app back is what makes the jump useful, and HopFocus does it without Accessibility or any other permission. It notes which app is active only at the moment you press a shortcut, and never reads window titles or contents.

Focus on Switch is free 10 times a day and unlimited for your first 7 days. Pro makes it unlimited.

Also

  • Remembers your spot on each display, so every jump lands where you were.
  • ⌃0 takes you back to where you were before the last jump (Pro).
  • More than two displays, each with its own shortcut (Pro).
  • Works with any arrangement, finds displays again after you replug, and tells identical monitors apart.
  • A ring shows where the pointer landed; Pro adds Spotlight, Ripple, Crosshair and Big arrow.
  • Jump to Display and Go Back in the Shortcuts app, Spotlight and Siri.

Asks for nothing

No Accessibility, Input Monitoring or Screen Recording permission. HopFocus runs in the App Sandbox, has no account, makes no network connections and collects no data. It reads the pointer position and the active app only at the moment you press a shortcut.
