Your pointer, on the right display.

HopFocus is a menu-bar utility for Mac. Press one shortcut and the pointer jumps to the display you mean, back to the exact spot where you last worked there.

Press ⌃1 for your built-in display and ⌃2 for your external one. With Pro, ⌃0 takes you back where you were. If you like, the app you were using on that display comes back to the front too, so you can type right away.

Asks for nothing

HopFocus needs no Accessibility, Input Monitoring or Screen Recording permission. It runs in the App Sandbox, has no account, makes no network connections and collects no data. It reads the pointer position only at the moment you press a shortcut.

Made for the Mac

  • Works with any arrangement of displays, and finds them again after you replug.
  • Choose how the pointer shows where it landed, from a quiet ring to a spotlight.
  • Also available in the Shortcuts app and Siri: Jump to Display and Go Back.
